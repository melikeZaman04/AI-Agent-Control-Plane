roadmap without routine confirmation. Never invent a new product roadmap.
Execute bounded task, test, self-review and repair loops. Obey material Human
Decision Gates; a missing verbose checklist alone is not a gate. Preserve user
changes. Verify exit criteria with actual evidence and update canonical docs.
Follow the current commit/push policy. Stop only at a real gate or the authorized
roadmap end; A4 product autonomy is not enabled.
